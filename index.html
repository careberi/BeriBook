      Chapter

      To use AI features, add your Claude API key in Settings. Everything runs from your device — your documents and key go straight to Claude, never through any other server.

      Settings

      Want a voice that sounds like a real person? Your device has free, natural “Enhanced” voices you can download once — they’ll then appear here (marked ⭐).

      On iPhone / iPad: Settings app → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English, then tap a voice and choose an Enhanced or Premium one to download (e.g. “Ava”, “Samantha”, “Zoe”). Come back to BeriBook and pick it from the list above.

      AI assistant (optional)

      The AI features (summaries, key points, Q&A) use your own Claude API key. Get one at console.anthropic.com. It’s stored only on this device and is sent directly to Claude — never to any BeriBook server. Leave blank to keep BeriBook fully offline.
      Install on your iPhone / iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen.” BeriBook then opens full-screen like a real app.
